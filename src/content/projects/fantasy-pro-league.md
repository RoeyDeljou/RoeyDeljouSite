## What it is

A tournament companion for the World Cup that combines **score prediction** with a **fantasy team** mode. Users predict match scores, join private prediction leagues or the global World League, follow live matches and climb XP leaderboards.

## Highlights

- Live World Cup fixtures and squads pulled from API-Football through serverless functions.
- Robust prediction UX: drafts persist locally and auto-save to the server, so a dropped connection never loses a prediction.
- Achievement and rivalry systems that keep users coming back between matchdays.
- Rapid, user-driven bug-fix cycles — shipped and fixed in days during a live tournament window, with Playwright tests in place.
