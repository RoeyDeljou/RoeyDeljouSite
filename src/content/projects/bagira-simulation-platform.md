## Context

Bagira Systems builds simulation-based training systems. Its platform ran on a legacy in-house game engine that limited realism, tooling and the speed at which new scenarios could be built.

## My role

- **Led the transition to Unity** — a major technology upgrade across the company's simulation platform, bringing modern rendering, tooling and an industry-standard workflow.
- **Built 3D simulation environments and combat/training scenarios** in Unity and C#.
- **Designed smarter simulation systems**: immersive animation pipelines, AI behaviours and high-performance mechanics that improved realism and flexibility.
- **Architected for scale and performance** in complex real-time training applications.
- **Introduced AI-assisted development workflows** and custom tooling that shortened iteration cycles for the team.

## Impact

The upgraded platform contributed to the company's expansion into the European market, including the deployment of its **first international simulator site**.
